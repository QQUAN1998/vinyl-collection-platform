## English

### A personal cabinet for physical records

For physical-record collectors and WeChat mini-program developers.

- Catalog records and attended shows
- Turn a collection into a ticket poster
- Two layouts: records and live shows

The visual tour uses local fixtures and never loads your WeChat collection. Running the mini program requires WeChat and the appropriate setup.

Visual tour: https://qquan1998.github.io/vinyl-collection-platform/

Source and setup: https://github.com/QQUAN1998/vinyl-collection-platform
